# Draft voices

Game voice clips indexed by the public DeadChaps draft sound catalog:
https://www.deadchaps.gg/api/deadlock/sounds

Original files: https://assets-bucket.deadlock-api.com/assets-api-res/sounds/vo/
Retrieved: 2026-10-30.

The bundled bank contains 548 selection clips across all 44 draft heroes and 499 unselection clips across 43 heroes. Sinclair uses the existing local ban-voice fallback because the catalog has selection lines, but no unselection lines for that character. Sinclair's Henry/Savannah variants and Nurse Harrow's internal `nurse` name are mapped explicitly.

Clips remain game audio belonging to their respective rights holders. No website-specific music or private assets were copied.
